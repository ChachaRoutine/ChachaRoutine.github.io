Chacha Routine Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Chacha Routine (the "App") does not collect any personal information. This policy explains what information the App stores and where.

1. Personal information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers. There is no sign-up or login, and the App does not send any information to the developer's servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information you enter in the App is stored only on your device:

  • Routine names, icons (emoji), repeat days, times, and start and end dates
  • Each routine's task list
  • Task check-offs and routine completion records by date
  • Reminder settings

This information is never sent to anyone, including the developer, and the developer cannot see it.

3. Reminders

Routine reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No external server is used to send them. Notification permission is used only if you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications.

To keep scheduled reminders up to date, the App may briefly run in the background when your device allows it. This also happens only on your device.

4. Sharing with third parties

The App does not share personal information with third parties or entrust its processing to anyone. The App does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

5. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the App's records may be included in that device backup. Such backups are managed according to Apple's policies and your settings, and the developer cannot access them.

6. Deleting information

When you delete a routine in the App, its records are deleted as well. When you delete the App from your device, all information stored by the App is deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page and in the App's update notes.
