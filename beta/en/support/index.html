Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting reminders

  • Make sure notifications are turned on in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > Chacha Routine.
  • If a Focus mode (such as Do Not Disturb) is on, reminders may arrive silently.
  • In the routine editor, check that "Remind Me at This Time" is turned on.
  • iPhone limits how many notifications one app can schedule in advance. If many routines have reminders, some may not arrive. Please turn off reminders for less important routines.

Where are my records stored?

All of your routines and records are stored only on your iPhone. There is no sign-up. Only when you turn on and use the AI coach are your messages to the coach and the routines and records you made with it sent so it can reply (see "AI coach" below).

How do I use the AI coach?

Open the Coach tab and write what you'd like to change. The coach asks a few questions and suggests routines. A routine is created only when you tap Add on its card, and you can tap Edit to change it first. Each goal gets its own conversation, and a weekly report on the past week arrives there.

Where does what I send to the AI coach go?

It goes through the Chacha Routine server to an AI in the US (Anthropic's Claude) to write a reply. The Chacha Routine server doesn't keep your conversations, and the AI provider deletes them within 30 days and doesn't train on them. Please don't include sensitive details such as your name, contact details, or health information. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I turn off the AI coach?

Turn off Settings > AI coach > Use the coach. Nothing is sent after that, and past conversations stay on your device. To delete a conversation, open the menu (⋯) in that coach conversation and tap Clear conversation.

The coach says that's all for today

The AI coach is free, so there's a daily limit on how many messages you can send. It resets at midnight UTC (9 AM in Korea). You can still add or edit routines from cards you've already received.

Will my records move to a new iPhone?

If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud backup or with device-to-device transfer, your records come along. Deleting the app deletes all of its records, so please be careful.

I forgot to check off a past day

Pick that day in the calendar at the top of the Home screen to check off its tasks. Your streak and stats are recalculated too.
