Chacha Routine Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 (release of version 1.1) The previous policy is summarized at the end of this page.

Chacha Routine (the "App") has no sign-up or login, and your routines and records are stored only on your device. Only if you turn on and use the AI coach (Chacha Coach) is the information below sent off your device so the coach can reply. If you don't turn on the coach, nothing leaves your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device:

  • Routine names, icons, repeat days, times, start and end dates, and task lists
  • Task check-offs, routine completion records by date, and reminder settings
  • Your conversations with the AI coach, goals and stages, and weekly reports (if you use the coach)

The developer cannot see this information.

2. Information sent when you use the AI coach

The coach asks for your consent the first time you use it, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > AI coach > Use the coach.

Details
What is sent Messages you send to the coach and your recent conversation with it (a summary for long conversations); the goals, stages, and routines you made with the coach (names, days, times, tasks) and their recent completion records; the names, days, and times of your other routines (so times don't overlap); the app language and today's date
What is not sent Your name, contact details, or location; the tasks and records of routines you didn't make with the coach; reminder settings
Purpose To write the coach's replies: routine suggestions, weekly reports, and stage-by-stage progress
When When you send the coach a message, and automatically when you open the App with the coach on, to receive weekly reports and stage results

We recommend not writing sensitive details such as your name, contact details, or health information in messages to the coach.

3. Information kept on our server

Conversations and routines pass through the Chacha Routine server to the AI and are not stored on the Chacha Routine server. Server logs do not contain conversation content.

The server keeps only the following, to prevent abuse:

Information Purpose Retention
Device attestation key (identifier and public key of a key created with Apple App Attest) To check that requests come from the Chacha Routine app While the coach service is operated (it contains nothing that identifies you; reinstalling the App creates a new key)
Daily usage counts Free usage limits Deleted automatically after 2 days

4. Processors and transfers abroad

To write the coach's replies, we entrust processing to the companies below, and information is transferred abroad. Nothing is transferred if you don't turn on the coach.

Recipient Country Information Purpose Retention
Anthropic, PBC (AI "Claude") United States The information in section 2 Writing the coach's replies Deleted automatically within 30 days of receipt; up to 2 years if automatically flagged for a usage policy violation. Anthropic does not use information received through its API to train its models
Vercel Inc. (server) United States The information in section 2 (passed through only), access logs Running the server Processed on the fly and not stored; access logs follow Vercel's policy
Upstash, Inc. (storage) United States The device attestation key and daily usage counts in section 3 Preventing abuse As in section 3
  • When and how: each time you use the coach, over encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • How to refuse, and what happens: don't turn on the coach, or turn it off in Settings. Only the AI coach becomes unavailable; routines, records, reminders, and all other features keep working.

The App does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

5. Reminders

Routine reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No external server is used to send them. Notification permission is used only if you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications.

To keep scheduled reminders up to date, the App may briefly run in the background when your device allows it. This also happens only on your device.

6. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the App's records may be included in that device backup. Such backups are managed according to Apple's policies and your settings, and the developer cannot access them.

7. Deleting information

  • When you delete a routine, its records are deleted as well.
  • You can delete a conversation or a goal from the menu in a coach conversation.
  • When you delete the App, all information stored by the App is deleted.
  • Information already sent to the AI is deleted automatically after the periods in section 4. Conversations are not kept on the Chacha Routine server, so there is nothing further for the developer to delete.

8. Children

The App does not ask for your age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. We recommend that children under 14 not use the AI coach without a parent's or guardian's consent.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page and in the App's update notes before it takes effect.
